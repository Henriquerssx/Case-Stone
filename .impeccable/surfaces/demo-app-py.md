## Scope
demo/app.py: the Impulso Stone engagement dashboard. Mode is **Operate**, shown live in a pitch: the evaluators are the primary audience (confirmed). Code-led build (Streamlit, Operate surface).

## Audience and task
- Evaluators should grasp the mechanism in one viewport: 6,675 real posts → 625 shine → what they share → a script tied to real posts.
- Then they watch the agent generate a storyboard live, in offline mode.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The dashboard is a night-sky constellation of small-business content. Every post is a star and the top 10% glow. It refuses the category default of white KPI tiles over stacked charts.

OWN-WORLD:
- Clube dos Sonhos grammar on an ink #03211D ground, with ink-soft #06352E panels and a mist #E6EAE9 1px hairline at 12%.
- Teal #32ADA1 uppercase pill actions with ink text. Impulso lime #C7F93C is reserved for "top 10% / shines", the glow.
- Outfit ExtraBold uppercase display at leading 0.95; Manrope body.
- A four-point star glyph with a mist glow.
- Real post covers under an ink bottom gradient.

STORY:
1. The evaluator reads the question, sees the constellation turn, and reads the one-line proof.
2. In the tabs, they see a niche "universe" chosen by its cover.
3. They generate a script and see the storyboard plus its source posts.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Full-width ink stage.
- Left 58%: the headline "O QUE FAZ UM PEQUENO NEGÓCIO BRILHAR?" (Outfit 800, uppercase, about 4.2 rem), a Manrope lede and an inline proof sentence with lime numerals.
- Right 42%: a rotating 3D four-point star on canvas, faces in lime/teal/deep, ringed by orbiting post-particles (625 lime out of 6,675 teal). It tilts with the pointer.
- The tab bar sits below as uppercase pills.

FORM: Constellation stage (user-pinned world: Clube dos Sonhos). Signature interaction: the pointer-tilted 3D star in the hero, plus a "Galáxia 3D" of all posts (Plotly scatter3d, top in lime) on the Como funciona tab. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
